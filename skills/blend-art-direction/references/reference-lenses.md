# Reading references without creating a house style

These lenses come from reference pages inspected on 2026-09-09. Sites change. Revisit the live source when exact current appearance or behavior matters. The observations are not implementation prescriptions, asset licenses, or claims about the sites' internal code.

## Claude Design: calm structure and expressive detail

The inspected [Claude Design page](https://claude.com/product/design) uses a light field, a prominent editorial serif heading, quieter sans-serif explanation, and a large media region. The useful relationship is the hierarchy: the words orient the visitor before the visual demonstration takes attention.

Transfer that separation of orientation and demonstration. Choose a different face, palette, alignment, or imagery when the brief warrants it. Do not make serif headlines, cream surfaces, hand-drawn accents, or identical hero proportions universal defaults. The page's media is not evidence that all of its effects are live DOM animation.

## Apple MacBook Neo: resolved product presentation

The inspected [MacBook Neo page](https://www.apple.com/eg/macbook-neo/) gives carefully framed hardware imagery substantial space, uses confident type, and presents product color and physical form as the visual subject. A product gallery includes explicit navigation/playback controls.

Transfer the investment in asset quality, product scale, crop, material coherence, and the relationship between claim and visual proof. Do not infer that a large animated-looking product requires WebGL. Choose live geometry only when interaction needs geometry and the available model can carry the required quality. A poor model is not elevated by being technically real-time.

## OpenClaw: a distinct technical and playful world

The inspected [OpenClaw homepage](https://openclaw.ai/) combines a dark field, orange character/accent, an ASCII-like animated visual area, large sans-serif copy with an italic counterpoint, and technical notation. The character and texture belong to the same world while the main action remains readable.

Transfer the coherence between audience, visual material, and movement. An atmospheric effect can convey identity without demonstrating a physical product. Do not transplant the terminal texture, orange mascot, dark palette, or mixed heading treatment into unrelated brands. Keep the foreground readable and bound sustained background work.

## A small mascot can act without dominating

The inspected [Claude mascot article](https://ayotomcs.me/claude-mascot) presents a small stepped character with readable face and prop poses. Its limited visual vocabulary makes the acting recognizable at modest scale.

Transfer silhouette economy, consistent proportions, pose readability, and grounded secondary motion. Do not treat a brand character as the default mascot for every app. Choose the user's character when supplied, or develop an original one that belongs to the app. A displayed animation does not prove integration with real application status.

## The supplied card recording: animate inside a stable frame

In the user's short recording, the text and card stay in place while a small grid-and-badge illustration changes. The badge contracts away, returns, and an arrow resolves within it. The sampled frames establish that local sequence; they do not establish the input trigger or precise easing.

Transfer the division of labor: static information remains easy to read, while a small illustration performs a coherent visual action. Another product might use a connector linking nodes, a folded route revealing a destination, or a pair of objects aligning. Those are possible interpretations, not required motifs. See blend-micro-motion for directing and implementing vignettes.

The supplied app screenshot offers a related static lesson: a small line illustration anchors a quiet empty state, with a clear heading, short explanation, and action. Its restrained scale leaves navigation and task orientation intact. The still image establishes composition, not whether the drawing animates. Empty states can carry character without becoming marketing heroes.

## Combine principles, not borrowed parts

The shared quality is resolved relationships: deliberate type scale, a strong visual subject, stable reading surfaces, controlled movement, and a recognizable identity. Their actual styles differ. A new design can adopt the calm hierarchy of one reference and the localized acting of another only if its own palette, shapes, line quality, type, and pacing make the combination coherent.

Before accepting a reference-derived choice, answer: what problem does it solve here, what must change for this product, and what would be wrong to copy? Keep the answer in working notes, not product UI. Do not ship captured reference images, recordings, or third-party character artwork as reusable suite assets.
