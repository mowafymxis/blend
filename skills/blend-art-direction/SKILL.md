# Blend Art Direction

Own visual character and composition. Produce decisions that can be seen in a render, not adjective lists or a menu of fashionable styles. In an established product, preserve its identity unless the user requests an overhaul.

For new or substantially changed identity, use [discovery](../blend-discovery/SKILL.md) before choosing. Ask about reference roles, desired closeness, dislikes, visual density, leading subject, theme coverage, and material character. Direct invocation does not bypass the interview. Reuse an existing decision record; do not re-ask settled choices. For light/dark systems, read [themes and surfaces](references/themes-and-surfaces.md).

## Interpret references at three levels

Separate appearance (scale, contrast, type, crop, spacing), behavior (trigger, sequence, pacing, interruption), and transferable principle (why attention or comprehension improves). Inspect the live page or supplied recording for behavior; a still establishes only appearance. Read [reference lenses](references/reference-lenses.md) when studying the suite's motivating examples. Do not infer hidden implementation from appearance.

Extract relationships instead of motifs. A calm frame around animated line art suggests a stable reading layer and a localized expressive layer. It does not require off-white backgrounds, a serif, hand drawing, or that exact icon. A polished product image suggests resolved lighting, scale, crop, and material fidelity; it does not require 3D or a particular manufacturer’s layout.

For a requested resemblance or an off-taste result, read [quality calibration](references/quality-bar.md). Match the reference at the same apparent scale: a small companion, a dominant product image, and a decorative icon have different jobs. Preserve the user's chosen style rather than treating “do not copy” as permission to ignore their visual preference. Transfer or reproduce the authorized visual relationships with content that belongs to the task.

## Choose a direction that has reasons

When taste is unresolved, describe or show a few meaningfully different directions and ask the user to choose or delegate. Distinguish them by content structure, hierarchy, type behavior, assets, and pacing, not only color. Use small specimens or storyboards when words are inadequate. Once a direction is selected, make routine composition decisions within it without repeated approval. Do not turn the user's request for a combination into a choice of only one reference.

Make the choice concrete: what dominates the first view; how reading proceeds; how sections change density and scale; what carries personality; what stays quiet. Choose the page's silhouette before isolated components. A centered hero can be right; so can a compact workspace, image field, editorial column, or asymmetric composition. No one is the default.

Use [composition and asset planning](references/art-direction.md) for content-to-layout decisions. Type is a structural choice: narrow display, broad grotesk, humanist UI, editorial serif, mono notation, and script each change the rhythm. Coordinate detailed type selection with blend-typography if available.

## Compose, do not decorate

Balance masses of type, image, and empty space. Set a dominant scale and purposeful counterpoint. Change section rhythm when the argument or task changes, retaining an alignment or material relationship that connects them. Cards belong where boundaries communicate independent items; open compositions belong where continuity matters. Neither is banned.

Use color to establish hierarchy, material, and state. Surface and shadow should agree with the lighting and depth model. A rounded container cannot rescue an unresolved crop. An oversized heading cannot rescue weak wording. Fix proportions and content before adding borders, gradients, particles, or animation.

Premium feel is contextual precision: convincing assets, controlled contrasts, deliberate crops, optical alignment, clear text, consistent behavior, and an absence of accidental gaps or jerks. It can be vivid, dense, playful, minimal, or highly illustrated. Avoid equating it with beige, oversized whitespace, black backgrounds, thin gray text, or luxury clichés.

## Review for coherence and range

Inspect the first view and the full page at actual reading size. Does the intended focal point win? Do quieter sections make stronger ones legible? Does mobile have its own composition? If everything competes, remove or reduce a treatment before adding another.

When several projects are available, compare their silhouettes, information architecture, type roles, visual mediums, and motion patterns. Similarity needs a shared product reason. Do not force novelty into a consistent brand family, and do not reuse the suite’s last successful combination merely because it worked once.

Handoff: a short direction and its visible implications, followed by implementation within scope. Preserve supplied language and brand choices; avoid turning design rationale into public interface copy.
