# Drawn animation and cartoon holds

Use this mode when the brief or supplied recording calls for editorial ink drawings with a cartoon animation feel. Inspect the recording over time, not only a screenshot or its SVG wrapper. Identify the action, silhouettes, changing contours, timing, contact points, and final hold. A Lottie file may be a sequence of drawings; the format itself does not establish the motion technique or quality.

Give each scene a readable visual verb and original subject. Design the established pose, anticipation, main gesture, overshoot and resolved pose. Keep supporting contacts anchored and connected strokes joined. Shape deformation and overlap should follow the object’s material. Rigid container bobbing, root rotation and indiscriminate point jitter do not reproduce illustrated-film motion.

Use confident curved contours, pressure variation, clear negative space and restrained colour. Small navigation icons need a separate optical treatment; preserve accepted icon animations when the request concerns only landing art. Avoid applying an illustration-size motion to every UI control.

For a requested continuing cartoon feel, complete the main action once and hold its result. A quiet cycle of a few slightly redrawn contour poses can keep that result alive. Sample and judge the sequence at its actual playback cadence; 12 fps or held cels can be useful, but neither is a universal requirement. Keep the outline variation small enough that the silhouette, contact points and semantic content remain stable. Do not replay the full story as the idle.

Create one convincing scene before propagating a new direction. Compare the reference and result at the same displayed size, including contact and transition frames. Changing renderer, file format, or animation service alone does not fix weak drawing. Do not keep replacing an accepted set after the user elects to retain it. If homepage and feature-page art must differ, author disjoint scene sets rather than recolouring the same drawings.

Respect the requested control treatment: do not add Replay or Pause buttons after the user has excluded them. Preserve reduced-motion and quiet-mode support, stop hidden/offscreen work, and release frame callbacks and listeners. Consider static or finite alternatives where sustained motion cannot have a suitable stopping mechanism; explain actual accessibility tradeoffs without silently overriding the brief.

Compose illustrations with the hero, not around accidental leftover space. Align their optical centres, preserve headline and CTA clearance, and check the top gap below navigation at wide and narrow sizes. At mobile widths, place the art in an intentional row instead of clipping large absolute-positioned drawings.

Verify the main episode, continuing hold, reduced motion, offscreen suspension and mobile composition. A generated recording is not proof it was inspected. Record remaining limits honestly.
