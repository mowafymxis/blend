# A brief the implementation can follow

This is internal working material, not a user-facing questionnaire or required form to complete. Record only useful decisions. Infer shape and feel from the brief/references; ask about consequential gaps. User-facing design Markdown contains only questions and answer fields.

## Purpose and boundaries

- Product, audience, primary task/action, intended feeling.
- Deliverable: design exploration, working prototype, production frontend, or questions only.
- Protected content, routes, integrations, brand assets, and existing behavior.
- Actual supplied evidence versus labeled sample content; missing assets and dependencies.
- Required languages, devices, accessibility constraints, stack, deadline, and asset budget.

Supported inferences and routine implementation choices do not need explicit delegation. Record their rationale without attributing them to the user. Wait on consequential questions actually asked; do not require every field to be answered. Preserve inherited constraints.

## Motion map

| Location and purpose | Subject / change | Trigger | Start, midpoint, finish | Replay / interruption | Quiet and mobile alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Only selected scenes | Describe a visible event | Entry, hover/focus, click, real event, ambient, or scroll | Include recognition hold | Finish, reverse, or retarget | Equivalent content and stable controls |

For cinematic scenes, add medium, asset readiness, scroll distance rationale, and start/release behavior. For mascots, add event priority and whether a state reflects a real operation or a clearly labeled demonstration. For atmosphere, add placement, density, rest/pause policy, and foreground contrast.

## First-draft acceptance

- Three highest-priority visible outcomes and what evidence will demonstrate each.
- Representative first section, card/vignette, mascot pose, or scroll beat to resolve before expanding.
- Explicitly excluded effects and unresolved blockers.
- Checks for the main action, agreed themes, type loading, mobile, reduced motion, and relevant interruption.

Maintain a short change note when an answer supersedes an earlier choice. Do not silently rewrite the decision record to match an already-built draft.
