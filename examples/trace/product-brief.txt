TRACE / PRODUCT CONCEPT

AI knowledge tools should make it easier to understand shared work and check the evidence behind an answer.

The proposed experience:
1. Bring relevant notes, plans, and decisions together.
2. Ask a question in ordinary language.
3. Read the answer and follow its references back to the source.

Design principles:
- Show sources beside the answer.
- Keep missing or unresolved information visible.
- Help people make decisions with context.

About this example:
Trace is a fictional AI-company concept built with Blend.
The included walkthrough uses fictional documents and prepared sample answers.
It does not connect to a live model, upload documents, or send data to a service.
Search across real workspaces, model inference, identity, permissions, and integrations are not implemented in this static example.
