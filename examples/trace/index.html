Show where it came from.−

Source references belong beside the answer. In the demo, every citation opens the sample document that supports it.

A missing decision is still missing. The sample answer for open questions points to unresolved work rather than inventing an outcome.

Keep people in the loop.+

Give people the context to make their own decisions. A summary is a starting point for review, not a substitute for judgment.
