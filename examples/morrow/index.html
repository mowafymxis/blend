Start with the work,
not the calendar.

A shorter week changes more than working hours. Separate the decision into three questions before choosing a pilot.

  1. What needs coverage? Map customer-facing hours and critical handovers.
  2. What can change? Audit meetings and recurring work before compressing the schedule.
  3. What would count as success? Compare service quality, workload, and team feedback.
