Common Ground

Opening architecture up to everyone.

Strategy · Identity · Print

A city is a shared space.

A self-initiated identity for a fictional architecture festival. The open doorway becomes a flexible graphic device: a frame for a programme, a meeting point on a poster, an invitation on a ticket. An electric palette makes a specialist subject feel public and approachable.

The idea
Make architecture an invitation, rather than an institution.
The system
An open arch, direct language and a three-colour print palette.

Good Measure

A little more care for the everyday cupboard.

Positioning · Identity · Packaging

Familiar, with feeling.

A fictional pantry brand built around the pleasure of everyday ingredients. Plainspoken labels, generous lettering and hand-shaped produce illustrations bring warmth to a consistent family of packs.

The idea
Make everyday staples feel considered, without making them precious.
The system
A recognisable label structure with ingredient-led colour and illustration.

A good identity is more than a good logo. It’s the way every part feels like it comes from the same place.

Format is a fictional independent design studio exploring that idea. We work across strategy, identity and digital experiences, bringing a clear thought all the way through to the smallest detail.
